# Connect Tel Customer Churn Analysis
 Connect Tel is a global leader in telecommunications, providing reliable voice, data, and Internet services. ConnectTel faces a critical challenge with customer churn, threatening its growth and sustainability. The company's current retention strategies are ineffective, leading to customer losses to competitors. To address this, ConnectTel has enlisted you as a Data Scientist to develop a customer churn prediction system. By applying advanced analytics and machine learning to customer data, the goal is to accurately predict churn and implement focused retention strategies. This approach will help reduce attrition, improve customer loyalty, and maintain a competitive edge in the fast-paced telecommunications market.
